# Brief: v0.6 — Moving, morphing and timed rounds

## Goal
Rounds 4, 5, 6, 8, 11 and 12 get their behavior. Uses the round `timeline` hook from v0.4 and the shape model from v0.5. Scene effects (doors, darkness, glitch, alert) are v0.7. Here, rounds 11 and 12 only get their shape and timing logic.

Update the "Gameplay rules" section of `CLAUDE.md` when done.

## 1. The rule for changing shapes
**Score against the shape exactly as it was displayed at the moment of the click.**
- On click, capture the geometry and position from the current animation frame, compute C, O and M from that snapshot, and store the snapshot in the round result (needed for the Details diagram).
- **Freeze on click** for every moving or morphing shape: the motion stops immediately, so the player sees what they clicked. (Owner asked for this on rounds 4 and 6; apply it to all for consistency.)
- Motion runs on `requestAnimationFrame`, starts at `round.shape.visible`, and pauses when the tab is hidden.

## 2. Timeouts
- Rounds can have `timeLimitMs`. When it expires without a click: emit `round.timeout`, record the round with no click (`q = 0`, excluded from the humanity lean and the mean latency), and continue to the outro and next round.
- The score screen's Details table shows "no input" for these rounds.
- Rounds can also have `inputWindows`: time ranges (from `shape.visible`) in which clicks are accepted. Clicks outside the windows are ignored.

## 3. The rounds

| Round | Shape | Behavior | Defaults |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Bean blob, about 360 × 220 | Continuously and slightly morphs: each control point drifts on smooth noise | Amplitude 5% of size, cycle about 3s |
| 5 | Triangular blob, about 320 × 280 | Morphs like round 4. **Decoy:** right after the shape is fully visible, an orange 2 × 2px dot blinks for 400ms (two on/off cycles) at the shape's **computed center C**, as a distraction toward the machine answer | `decoyTarget: 'computed'` (can switch to `'optical'`) |
| 6 | Oval, about 240 × 150 | Moves left and right on a soft wave and loops: `x = A·sin(ωt)`, `y = B·sin(2ωt + φ)`. The full shape always stays inside the screen with a margin, never touching the edges | Margin 48px, A computed from the available width, B = 40px, period about 4s |
| 8 | Complex star, about 300 × 300 | Irregular 7-point star (varied inner radii). Jumps to a new random position every 600ms, inside the safe area. Instant jump, no animation in between | `jumpEveryMs: 600`, positions from the seeded RNG |
| 11 | Rectangle | Shrinks from 200 × 200 to 40 × 40 over 10s, centered. **Deadline:** no click by 10s → timeout and the game continues. `falloffRadius` follows the current size, so later clicks are judged more strictly | Linear shrink, `timeLimitMs: 10000` |
| 12 | Smiley cutout (from v0.5) | Visible 1000ms, then hidden (fade-out over the last 200ms). **Clicks count for 5000ms total**: during the 1s visible plus 4s afterwards. Then 4000ms where input is ignored. No click → timeout | `inputWindows: [[0, 5000]]`, `postRoundIdleMs: 4000` |

Latency always counts from `shape.visible`.

## 4. Details diagram on the score screen
Draw each round's click on that round's own snapshot (small multiples, one per round) instead of one shared rectangle, since the shapes now differ. Hovering a click shows the shared tooltip.

## 5. Debug panel
- Pause/resume motion, step one frame, and show live C/O markers on moving shapes.
- Show the remaining time and active input window for rounds 11 and 12.

## Done when
- Each listed round behaves as described. The snapshot scoring test passes: a click during motion is scored against the frame that was displayed.
- Rounds 4, 5, 6 and 8 freeze on click.
- The decoy dot appears on C for round 5 only, once.
- Round 6 never touches the screen edges at the design size.
- Round 11 times out at 10s and continues. Round 12 accepts clicks only during its 5s window.
- Timeouts show as "no input" and score 0. Tests and build pass.

## Out of scope
Doors closing, darkness, glitch, alert, background morph, screen drop (all v0.7).
