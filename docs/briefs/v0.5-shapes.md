# Brief: v0.5 — Shape library and static rounds

## Goal
Rounds get their real shapes. This brief covers every shape that does not move: rounds 1, 2, 3, 7, 9, 10 (shape only) and 12 (shape only). Moving and timed behavior is v0.6; scene effects are v0.7.

All sizes in design px (1rem = 16px). Update the "Gameplay rules" section of `CLAUDE.md` when done.

## 1. Shape model (`src/rounds/shapes/`)
- A shape is `{ outer: Point[], holes: Point[][] }`: closed polygons, densely sampled (enough points that curves look smooth, around 1 point per 3px of perimeter).
- Generators are pure functions of `(params, rng)` so seeded shapes are reproducible. Each generator lives in its own file and has a unit test.
- Render as one SVG `<path>` with `fill-rule="evenodd"`, so holes are real cutouts.
- Shape transforms (offset from the screen center, rotation) are applied in one place, and the geometry used for scoring is the transformed geometry.

## 2. Optical center for shapes with holes
Update `opticalCenter.ts` (and its tests):
- **C (computed center)** = area centroid of the actual material: the outer shape minus its holes.
- **O (optical center)** is computed from the **outer contour only**, as if the holes were filled. O may fall inside a hole, and that is correct (owner decision).
- The fallback "if O is outside the shape → use M" checks against the filled outer contour.
- Tests: ring/avocado (O can sit in the hole; C sits in the material), smiley (eyes and mouth don't affect O).

## 3. Per-round falloff
`falloffRadius` defaults to 0.5 × the shorter side of the shape's on-screen bounding box. Allow a per-round override in the config (needed for the very large shape in round 7).

## 4. The shapes

| Round | Shape | Size (bounding box) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rectangle | 360 × 360 | No rotation, centered |
| 2 | Random blob | about 500 × 300 | Seeded per game: 8–10 points around an ellipse with varied radii, joined by a smooth closed curve. May be concave; must never cross itself (test this across 500 seeds) |
| 3 | Avocado with a hollow center | about 280 × 380, upright | Pear outline: a smaller top bulb flowing into a larger bottom bulb with a smooth waist. A circular hole (the pit, about 35% of the width) in the lower bulb |
| 7 | Large rectangle | Fills the free screen area | Keep `largeShapeMargin` (default 40px) from the HUD, the objective text and the screen edges. Size computed from `layout.config.ts`, not hard-coded |
| 9 | Perfect circle | 320 × 320 | Centered |
| 10 | Asymmetric rhombus | about 300 × 220 | Four corners with unequal angles. Rotated about 14°, offset about 120px left of center. The screen "drop" itself is v0.7 |
| 12 | Smiley face cutout | 300 × 300 | Paper-colored disc with two eye holes and a mouth hole (a curved band) cut out |

Rounds 4, 5, 6, 8 and 11 keep the 200 × 200 placeholder rectangle until v0.6.

## 5. Debug panel
- Show C, O and M markers on every shape.
- A "reroll" button for round 2 (new seed) and a shape gallery view that shows all shapes at once, with their markers, for visual review.

## Git
Branch `feat/v0.5-shapes`, PR, preview link, owner merges, then version `0.5.0` and tag.

## Done when
- Each listed round shows its shape at the correct size and position. The gallery view shows all of them with markers.
- Round 2 blobs never cross themselves; the same seed gives the same blob.
- Holes render as cutouts, and the O marker can sit inside the avocado's pit.
- Round 7's rectangle respects the margins at the design size.
- Tests and build pass.

## Out of scope
Morphing, movement, timing windows, decoy dot, glitch, screen drop, scene effects.
