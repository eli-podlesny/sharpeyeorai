# Brief: v0.7 — Scene effects

## Goal
The environment reacts across the game: the background starts breathing, the screen glitches, the alarm kicks in, the screen drops, the lights go out. After this brief all 12 rounds are complete, and merging it makes **v1.0**.

Effects run on the placeholder art for now and will be retuned when real assets arrive in v1.1. Every number below lives in config.

Update `CLAUDE.md` when done: allow WebGL for the background layer (see section 1), and record the scene-mode timeline in "Gameplay rules".

## Scene-mode timeline

| When | Scene mode | Effects active |
|---|---|---|
| Ready → round 3 | `normal` | None |
| After round 3's outro | `distorted` | Background breathing, subtle |
| Round 7 | `distorted` | + screen glitch: 200ms every 1400ms |
| After round 7's outro | `alert` | Alert pulse + "System Message" popup; background breathing becomes faster and stronger |
| Round 9 | `alert` | + screen glitch: 800ms glitch, 400ms calm, repeating |
| Round 10 | `alert` | + the screen drops |
| Round 11 | `blackout` (ramping in) | The doors close over the 10s deadline; the scene darkens toward near-black |
| Round 12 | `blackout` | Darkness; only the round's own content is visible |
| End | `normal` | Lights return, effects off, the doors open on the score |

The scene mode drives the effects through a single controller (`src/fx/sceneController.ts`) that listens to round events. No effect code inside round files.

## 1. Background breathing (WebGL, background layer only)
- A small WebGL fragment shader (no library) displaces the background image with slow, smooth noise, like seeing it underwater. It's a living, breathing motion, not a wobble.
- Two presets: `subtle` (displacement about 4px, slow) and `strong` (about 10px, about 2× faster). Transitions between presets ease over 2s.
- Fallback: if WebGL is unavailable, or `prefers-reduced-motion` is set, show the static image.
- Must hold 60fps on a mid-range laptop: one draw call, the image uploaded once as a texture, and the canvas sized to the layer.

## 2. Screen glitch (`src/fx/glitch.ts`)
- Affects everything inside the screen: the screen background, timer, progress bar, objective text and the shape. Not the frame, doors or HUD outside the screen.
- The look while active: 2–4 horizontal slices shifted sideways by random amounts, brief jitter, a slight opacity drop, and a noise overlay. Monochrome only, no color splitting.
- **Visual only:** the shape's real position used for scoring does not change. Clicks during a glitch work normally.
- Safety: no more than 3 full flashes per second at any time.

## 3. Alert mode (`src/fx/alert.ts`)
- The scene background color animates softly between `#111` and hazard orange, a smooth pulse with a period of about 2.4s. No hard flashing.
- An ellipse sized to 100% of the viewport width and height, layered above all scene layers, takes the same animated color at 16% opacity, with `pointer-events: none`.
- At the moment alert begins, the "System Message: ALERT!" box from the Figma Drama frame appears in the top-right of the screen for 4s, then hides before round 8's intro. (This keeps it from clashing with the sample tooltip, which also lives top-right.)

## 4. Screen drop (round 10)
- During round 10's intro, the whole screen assembly (frame, doors, screen, glow) drops: it moves toward the left of the viewport, rotates about −8°, and scales to 0.8, so it no longer fits fully in view. A heavy mechanical ease, with a small settle at the end.
- Clicks must map correctly into the transformed screen: compute the inverse of the assembly's actual transform matrix in `toStageCoords` / the shape-local conversion. Do not rely on bounding rectangles, which break with rotation. Unit test it.
- The assembly returns to its normal position during round 10's outro.

## 5. Blackout (rounds 11–12)
- **Round 11:** the doors close gradually over exactly the 10s deadline (fully closed at 10s). An overlay darkens the scene from 0 to 90% over the same 10s. Clicks still count until the deadline, even where a door already covers the screen (map them to screen coordinates as usual).
- **Between 11 and 12:** the doors snap open while the scene is dark, out of sight. The scene stays at 90% darkness.
- **Round 12:** the intro text and smiley are shown at full brightness within the dark scene. After the smiley disappears, the screen goes fully dark for the rest of the round.
- **End:** during the last 4s of idle time, the doors close. Then the lights return to normal, every effect switches off, and the doors open revealing the score.

## Done when
- A full game runs through the timeline above with no console errors and a steady frame rate.
- Clicks on round 10's dropped screen score correctly (tested).
- The glitch never changes scoring, and never exceeds 3 flashes per second.
- Reduced-motion mode disables breathing and shortens the drop, while the game remains playable.
- Tests and build pass.

## Out of scope
Final art, sound (events are already emitted), polish animations, the photosensitivity notice (v1.2).
